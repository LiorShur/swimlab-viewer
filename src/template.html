          Per‑breath metrics

          —
          Breath (t, s)SideΔpitch °peak roll ° roll:pitchduration s
          Δpitch = peak pitch in the breath window minus the median over the preceding non‑breath cycle. Excluded breaths (within 4 s of a push‑off or 2 s of trial end) are kept and marked, never dropped silently.
          swimlab — offline analysis of a head‑mounted IMU (Movella DOT, occipital mount, 120 Hz). Angles are gravity‑referenced relative to the T0b prone calibration pose; yaw and magnetometer are unusable in pool halls and are never used.
